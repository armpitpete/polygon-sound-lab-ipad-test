## Accepted scope

The Metallic voice's current FM/percussion synthesis and six factory presets: Distant Bell, Alloy Spark, Machine Tooth, Ice Chime, Rust Plate, Glass Engine. The existing Pitch, Tone, Texture, Attack, Decay and Level controls are retained. Individual preview, preset workflow and WAV export remain features of the experimental Sound Lab.

Technical review reported 40/40 checks passed; no Metallic DSP edits are required as a consequence of this approval.

## Limits and change control

This is a **user acceptance of the Metallic voice as reviewed**, not a full Sound Lab acceptance, not proof of real Windows-PC acceptance of the Sound Lab, and not authorisation to change or integrate with the established Polygon percussion sequencer.

The Noise voice was separately accepted at v0.1.5 and remains frozen. Sub Pulse and Organic have not yet been accepted.

**Freeze Metallic v0.1.5.** Any future Metallic sound changes require a new isolated revision and separate review. No existing Sound Lab sound code should change as part of documenting this PASS.
