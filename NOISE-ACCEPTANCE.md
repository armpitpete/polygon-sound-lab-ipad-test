## Accepted Noise features

No unintended pitched oscillator.

## Scope of acceptance

This is **the user's listening PASS for the Noise voice**, not approval of all four Sound Lab voices, not Windows PC acceptance of the complete Sound Lab, not iPad validation of every storage/export workflow, and not acceptance or modification of the established Polygon percussion sequencer.

**Change control:** Preserve this accepted Noise implementation and sound semantics. Any further Noise fixes or features require a new isolated revision and separate acceptance. No integration into the main sequencer is authorised by this PASS.
